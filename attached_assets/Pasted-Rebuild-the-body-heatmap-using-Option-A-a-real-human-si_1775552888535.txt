- Keep front and back bodies aligned horizontally
- Give them enough space so they do not feel cramped
- Make labels like FRONT and BACK small and subtle

- Premium, minimal, dark-mode athletic UI
- Think modern performance app, not medical app and not gaming UI
- Avoid cartoon anatomy, round blobs, or exaggerated muscle shapes
