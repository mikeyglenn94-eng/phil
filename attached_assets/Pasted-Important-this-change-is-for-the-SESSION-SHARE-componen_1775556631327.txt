Redesign the SESSION SHARE card from scratch with a much simpler, progress-based format.

GOAL:
Make session share feel premium, clear, and worth sharing by focusing on the best performance from that specific workout.

SESSION SHARE RULES:
- This is about one workout only
- No weekly summary
- No body heatmap
- No complicated visuals
- No anatomy graphics
- No charts unless extremely minimal
- Focus on one clear session win

Use this structure for SESSION SHARE only:

1. Top row
- Session type on the left, e.g. SBD
- Date on the right, small and subtle

2. Main hero section
- Exercise name as the key movement from that session
- Weight × reps as the largest element on the card
  Example:
  Bench Press
  60kg × 10

3. Supporting session progress metrics
Use only 2 to 3 of these max:
- Estimated 1RM
- Change vs last time this exercise was performed
- Rep PR at this weight
- Volume for this lift today
- Average reps across work sets for this lift today

4. Optional one-line insight
Examples:
- +5kg vs last Bench Press session
- Rep PR at this load
- Highest estimated 1RM this month
Keep it short and tied to this session only

5. Visual style
- Premium dark background is fine for session share
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Plenty of spacing
- No squashed lines
- No boxes inside boxes
- No body diagram
- No gimmicks

6. Layout priority
The visual focal point should be:
- the exercise
- the weight × reps
Everything else should support that

7. Important protection
Do not apply this redesign to:
- dashboard share
- weekly summary share
- progress share
- any existing dashboard components

Only replace the current session share card shown in Share Activity.

Success criteria:
- instantly readable in 1 to 2 seconds
- clearly tied to one training session
